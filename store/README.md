**Screenshots** are 2560 × 1600, one of the Mac sizes App Store Connect accepts (16:10), taken of the
real app on macOS 27 (not mock-ups), five per language:

1. Seasoned, with the table of contents open
2. Rendered
3. Search every document (⇧⌘F), with results
4. Dark, with the per-syntax palette and the body font
5. Settings

The notes in them are written for the purpose, one per language; the search results are three
companion notes that exist only for the screenshot. Nothing personal is on screen.
