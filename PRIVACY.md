# Privacy

marktile collects nothing about you. It is a document editor that runs on your Mac, and that is
all it does.

- **No account, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising.** marktile has no server to send
  anything to.
- **Your documents stay where they are.** marktile reads and writes the files you open and the
  folders you allow, on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded or copied elsewhere.
- **Search runs on your Mac.** It uses the Spotlight index already on your Mac and reads files
  directly. What you search for is not recorded and goes nowhere.
- **What marktile remembers stays on your Mac**: the folders you have allowed it to read, your text
  size and toolbar choice. They live in the app's own storage and are removed with the app.
- **Links open in your browser.** ⌘-clicking a web link hands it to your default browser; marktile
  itself makes no network requests.
- **Crash reports** reach us only if you have turned on *Share with app developers* in System
  Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Analytics & Improvements. That is Apple's setting, and those
  reports are handled under Apple's privacy policy.

If this ever changes, this page changes first, and its history is public in this repository.
